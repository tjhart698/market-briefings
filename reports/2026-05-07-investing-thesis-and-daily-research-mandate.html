Carve Market Desk

Investing Thesis and Daily Research Mandate

Published May 7, 2026 · Six-month mandate for a $5,000 Public brokerage sleeve

This paper defines the operating framework for Carve’s autonomous equity research and trade recommendation process: concentrated positions, hard risk limits, disciplined execution, daily market/sector/company research, and approval required before every trade.

Mandate Summary

Portfolio Rules

  • Hard max allocation: $5,000 total
  • Equities only
  • BITB allowed as the lone crypto-adjacent exception, capped at 20%
  • No options, derivatives, margin, shorting, or direct crypto
  • Concentrated portfolio: normally 3–5 positions

Execution Rules

  • Preflight every order before submission
  • Approval required before every trade
  • Buy limits at the bid or only $0.01–$0.05 above it
  • Sell limits at the ask or only $0.01–$0.05 below it
  • Prefer liquid names and avoid chasing spreads

Five Investor Lessons Applied

Warren Buffett

Treat stocks as pieces of real businesses and favor understandable companies with durable competitive advantages.

Benjamin Graham

Protect downside first and require a margin of safety between market price and business value.

Peter Lynch

Keep the thesis simple, verifiable, and rooted in business reality rather than story-driven hype.

Howard Marks

Risk management and cycle awareness matter as much as stock selection, especially in concentrated portfolios.

George Soros

Stay flexible when facts change; if thesis, trend, or positioning breaks, adapt fast rather than defend ego.

How Those Lessons Shape the Trading Plan

  • Favor liquid, high-quality or high-momentum equities with a clear catalyst.
  • Keep initial positions roughly in the $900–$1,250 range, with single-name exposure controlled.
  • Avoid activity for activity’s sake; no trade is better than a weak trade.
  • Allow exits at a loss when thesis, price action, news, or opportunity cost changes materially.
  • Benchmark against the S&P 500 and avoid underperforming by more than 10 percentage points over the six-month window.

Daily Mandatory Research Framework

1. Market Trend Research

  • S&P 500, Nasdaq, Russell, VIX, rates, dollar, breadth, and volume
  • Trend state: bullish, neutral, or defensive
  • Macro risks: Fed, earnings calendar, inflation, geopolitics, liquidity

2. Sector Trend Research

  • Relative strength and weakness by sector
  • Rotation, leadership changes, and concentration risk
  • Where capital is flowing and where it is leaving

3. Company-Specific Research

  • Earnings revisions, guidance, valuation, news, ownership signals, and technical structure
  • What strengthens the thesis and what invalidates it
  • Clear catalyst, entry logic, and exit logic before capital is committed

Pre-Trade Decision Standard

  1. Is the business or setup understandable?
  2. Is there a clear edge, catalyst, or asymmetry?
  3. Is downside defined and acceptable within the mandate?
  4. Does this improve the portfolio versus current alternatives?
  5. Can the thesis be explained clearly in a short approval memo?

Bottom Line

Carve Market Desk will combine business quality, valuation discipline, risk control, and tactical flexibility. The objective is not maximum trade count. The objective is selective, well-researched, high-conviction equity positioning under strict process controls.
