Trading Operations Protocol

Published May 8, 2026 · Trading cadence, guardrails, and journal workflow

This protocol locks in the operating schedule for Tyler’s equity-only trading project: fixed review times, 15-minute heartbeats during active hours, major-issue-only escalation, and a persistent closeout journal published into the Market Briefings archive.

Mandate Snapshot

  • Account: BROKERAGE 5OS85748
  • Hard max allocation: $5,000
  • Allowed: equities only, with BITB as the sole exception capped at 20%
  • Not allowed: options, derivatives, crypto, shorting, or margin
  • Portfolio shape: concentrated, usually 3-5 positions
  • Approval required before every trade

Daily Operating Schedule

8:00 AM CT

Pre-market prep covering overnight macro, futures, rates, volatility, and any material news affecting holdings or the active watchlist.

8:30 AM CT

Open checklist and no-impulse reminder to avoid chasing price or widening spreads casually at the open.

12:00 PM CT

Midday risk review focused on market tone, breadth, volatility, and holding-specific developments.

2:30 PM CT

Late-day risk review to catch volatility shifts, material holdings news, or changing close dynamics.

3:00 PM CT

Closeout journal prompt and website update covering trades taken or considered, rationale, P/L, mistakes, and lessons for tomorrow.

Heartbeat Rules

  • Heartbeat cadence: every 15 minutes during active hours only
  • Active hours: 8:30 AM-3:00 PM Central Time
  • Skip weekends and market holidays automatically
  • Interrupt only for major issues

Major issues means macroeconomic news that changes the setup, significant news affecting an existing holding, or a broad market volatility spike.

Execution Discipline

  • Prefer limit orders; market orders only for very liquid names
  • Buy limits at the bid or only $0.01-$0.05 above it
  • Sell limits at the ask or only $0.01-$0.05 below it
  • Avoid chasing price and paying the spread unnecessarily
  • Losses may be realized when thesis, price action, news, or opportunity cost changes materially

Bottom Line

This system is designed for disciplined research, risk review, journaling, and trade recommendation support. It is not authorized for autonomous order placement.
